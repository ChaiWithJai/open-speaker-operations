AI Engineer speaker guide

Write a proposal reviewers can champion

Make a specific promise to a specific audience. Give a human reviewer enough evidence to picture the session, assess its fit, and trust you can deliver it.

Start with the audience outcome

Write one sentence: “After this session, a [level] attendee who [current situation] can [observable outcome].” Use it to decide what belongs in the abstract.

Build the abstract in four moves

  1. Name the real problem and who experiences it.
  2. Explain what the session will demonstrate or teach.
  3. Give two or three concrete takeaways.
  4. State the assumed experience level.

Prefer a title with a concrete tension, result, or surprising constraint. Avoid unexplained product names and inflated claims.

Fit the program

Choose one format and one to five topics. Production case studies, technical deep dives, live builds, evaluations, incident lessons, research-to-practice translations, and evidence-backed points of view all fit.

Commercial-content policy: vendor-affiliated proposals cannot be vendor-only main-stage talks. Disclose the relationship and choose an appropriate workshop, leadership, expo, or booth context. Every proposal receives human review.

Make a selective program easier to assemble

At a 5–15% acceptance rate, strong proposals still lose to a clearer fit or a more complementary program mix. Scope the claim, disclose constraints, say what is genuinely novel, and show what an attendee can apply afterward. Multiple submissions are welcome when they are genuinely distinct.

What happens next

A human reviewer scores program fit, speaker value, and practicality. The chair retains decision authority. Acceptances go out in waves on August 15, September 1, and September 15.

Selected speakers receive free event admission, economy travel, and accommodation guidance (two domestic nights or three international nights). The team collects an MC introduction so stage time stays focused, then professionally records and publishes the session. Strong contributors may be invited back in a future program.
